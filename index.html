常见问题Questions

可以用自己的角色图或宠物照吗？Can I use my own artwork or pet photos?

可以。打开浮光的「主题与背景」，点「添加背景」，选择不超过 10 MiB 的 PNG、JPEG 或 WebP。想从图里取色或调整焦点，可以在「背景工坊」继续制作。Yes. Open Shimmer’s theme library and choose Add Wallpaper. Pick a PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 10 MiB. To sample colors or adjust the focal point, open the image in Wallpaper Studio.

可以在浮光里把照片做成动图吗？Can I turn a photo into an animation in Shimmer?

现在可以直接导入动画 WebP 作为背景。试试首页雨夜的 2.5D 效果。鼠标停着也能看见落雨，轻轻一移，伞和街景就会错开。 我们计划在后续桌面版加入「图片转动图」，让你在浮光里把自己的照片或插画做成动态背景。You can already import an animated WebP as a background. Try the rainy street in 2.5D here. The rain falls on its own. Move the pointer and the umbrella shifts against the street. We plan to add a way to animate your own photos and illustrations in the desktop app.

浮光支持哪些应用和系统？Which app and platforms does Shimmer support?

浮光为官方 Codex 桌面应用提供主题。Mac 支持 Apple 芯片和 Intel 机型，系统需为 macOS 13 或更高版本。Windows 需为 Windows 10 或更高版本的 x64 系统，并使用 Microsoft Store 版 Codex。Shimmer themes the official Codex desktop app. It supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. On Windows, use the Microsoft Store edition of Codex on a Windows 10 or later x64 system.

导入主题会马上换掉当前的背景吗？When does an imported theme take effect?

导入的主题会先加入本机主题库。选中它可以先看预览，等你点「应用到 Codex」，当前窗口才会换上新主题。Importing adds the theme to your local library. Select it to preview the result. Your Codex window switches when you choose Apply to Codex.

怎样回到 Codex 原来的样子？How do I get back to the original Codex appearance?

从 Mac 菜单栏或 Windows 系统托盘打开浮光，关闭背景即可暂停主题，收藏的图仍在本机。需要完全恢复时，可选择恢复功能，按提示确认关闭 Codex 并移除浮光。Turn off the background from the Mac menu bar or Windows system tray to pause the theme. Your saved images stay in the library. For a full restore, choose the restore option and follow the prompt to close Codex and remove Shimmer.
